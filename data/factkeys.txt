# Left: question shapes ("who founded" for "who founded Microsoft", a bare
# word for "the currency of Japan"); right: fact box row names, lowercase. A
# row matches when its name equals one of them or starts with it and a space
# ("population" matches "population total"). Shapes starting with when or how,
# and the measured words below, only accept values that contain a number.
