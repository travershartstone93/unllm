# A leading ~ means the phrase may appear anywhere in the question; plain
# triggers must be the whole question. Triggers and questions are compared
# after normalization, so endings and punctuation do not matter.
# The trigger families come from the questions people have typed at chatbots
# since ELIZA; the replies are this library's own, and they are fixed:
# same question, same answer, every time.
